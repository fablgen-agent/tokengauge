## Audit, evidence, and deduplication

I first read 'src/data/research-methods.json' (85 entries) and headings in 'research/agents/04-methods-cache-context.md', '05-methods-prompt-output-tools.md', and '06-methods-routing-batch-evals.md'. **Count:** 250 candidates across ten families. Each row is one intervention boundary with its own action, measurement, and caveat. If later review finds a semantic duplicate, drop it rather than renaming it.

**Dedupe method:** lowercase and normalize title/action text, remove punctuation and provider names, compare 3-gram overlap with all existing IDs/titles/headings, then manually inspect the nearest family. IDs luna-001… are stable within this research file and are the proposed canonical IDs.

**Evidence grades:** all rows are conservatively marked **derived**: the linked first-party provider documentation or original paper supports the mechanism, while the exact application-level savings must be measured. I live-validated representative OpenAI prompt/token/tool/structured-output/eval/embedding/vision docs, Anthropic context docs, Gemini structured-output docs, AWS Bedrock cache/logging docs, and original LLMLingua, LongLLMLingua, RAG/REALM/ColBERT/SPLADE/HyDE, ReAct/Toolformer/DSPy/FrugalGPT, LoRA/QLoRA/GPTQ/AWQ/PagedAttention/FlashAttention, and HELM papers on 2026-08-15.
