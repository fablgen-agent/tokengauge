## Core copy

Name: TokenGauge

Tagline: Know what every model call costs.

Short description (260 characters):

> Compare official rates across nine AI providers, model realistic token-cost changes, and run controlled A/B tests with ChatGPT or your own provider keys. Prompts and outputs are not stored.

Launch offer:

> Launch 100: the first 100 authenticated identities can buy Pro for £5, Pro+ for £15, or Ultimate for £20. These are one-time purchases. Normal prices are £9, £19, and £39. API usage is billed separately by the connected provider.

Maker introduction:

> I built TokenGauge because AI cost advice is usually either a stale pricing table or a list of tips with no quality check. TokenGauge keeps provider-specific pricing details visible, labels evidence strength, and lets you compare supported methods with paired tests. The free surface includes current rate cards, a scenario calculator, 12 open evidence cards, and controlled lab recipes. Paid access unlocks the complete catalogue, provider connections, and deeper experiment history. I would especially value feedback on which provider-specific billing rule is easiest to get wrong in a real workload.

Independence disclosure:

> TokenGauge is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or any listed provider. “Continue with ChatGPT” uses the open-source `@opencoredev/loginwithchatgpt` integration rather than an official OpenAI OAuth product.
